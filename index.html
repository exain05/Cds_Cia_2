Global Food Price & Commodity Market Analysis

Exploring how food prices changed across countries, commodities, food categories and market types from 2019 to 2026.

Interactive Data Story

This project uses World Food Programme Global Food Prices data to examine changes in observed food prices before, during and after the COVID-19 period. The visualizations below explore global trends, major price shocks, geographic differences, commodities, categories, market types and price volatility.

01 — The Global Price Story

1. Global Median Food Prices Over Time

The overall movement of median observed food prices across the study period.

2. The Global Food-Price Shock

Food prices indexed to 2019 = 100 to highlight the scale of the major price increase.

3. Year-on-Year Change in Global Food Prices

Annual percentage changes reveal when price growth accelerated or slowed.

4. Food Prices Across Major Historical Periods

Comparison of major periods surrounding the COVID-19 shock and subsequent price changes.

02 — Where Did Prices Change?

5. Global Food-Price Hotspots

Country-level differences in median observed food-price changes across the selected period.

03 — What Changed?

6. Commodities With the Largest Price Changes

Highlights commodities that experienced the largest changes in observed prices.

7. Food-Price Change by Category

Compares price movements across major food categories.

04 — How Did Markets Behave?

8. Retail vs Wholesale Food Prices

Comparison of observed food-price patterns between retail and wholesale markets.

9. Most Volatile Food Commodities

Identifies commodities with the greatest variability in observed USD prices.

05 — Understanding the Dataset

10. Expansion of the Food-Price Dataset

Shows how the countries and commodities represented in the dataset changed across the study period.
